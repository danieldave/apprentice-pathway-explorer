Bead Making & Bead Jewellery

Make bead necklaces, bracelets, bags and bridal sets for weddings and fashion.

Your first step

Make one bracelet and one necklace this week with a simple bead kit.

Summary of the work

Make bead necklaces, bracelets, bags and bridal sets for weddings and fashion.

Known clusters: Bida (Niger State) for glass beads.

Skills training

  • Bead types and colours
  • Stringing and knotting
  • Bridal coral sets
  • Beaded bags and shoes
  • Pricing and sales

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepMake one bracelet and one necklace this week with a simple bead kit.
  2. Learn beads and tools
  3. String simple bracelets
  4. Make necklaces and earrings
  5. Make a bridal set
  6. Sell online and at events

Certification

Portfolio, exhibitions and craft fairs; trade test where offered.
