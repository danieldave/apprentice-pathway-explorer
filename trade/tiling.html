Tiling & Floor Finishing

Lay floor and wall tiles in homes, offices and shops with straight lines, clean cuts and level surfaces.

Your first step

Ask a tiler you trust if you can follow them to one job this week. Watch how they set out the first line before laying any tile.

Summary of the work

Lay floor and wall tiles in homes, offices and shops with straight lines, clean cuts and level surfaces.

Skills training

  • Setting out and levelling floors
  • Cutting tiles with hand and electric cutters
  • Mixing and spreading tile adhesive
  • Grouting and finishing
  • Estimating tiles per square metre

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepAsk a tiler you trust if you can follow them to one job this week. Watch how they set out the first line before laying any tile.
  2. Learn tile types, tools and safety
  3. Practise cutting and spacing tiles
  4. Tile a small room with your master
  5. Do wall tiling, steps and edges
  6. Price a full job and buy materials
  7. Work on your own and build referrals

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) through the Federal Ministry of Labour or your state examinations board; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate.
