Laundry & Dry Cleaning

Wash, dry-clean, iron and deliver clothes for busy homes, students and hotels.

Your first step

Work at a busy laundry for two weeks and learn how they tag and track every customer’s clothes.

Summary of the work

Wash, dry-clean, iron and deliver clothes for busy homes, students and hotels.

Skills training

  • Fabric care and stain removal
  • Machine operation
  • Ironing and folding
  • Customer tracking and tagging
  • Pickup and delivery

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepWork at a busy laundry for two weeks and learn how they tag and track every customer’s clothes.
  2. Learn fabrics and stain removal
  3. Work at a laundry
  4. Operate machines and press
  5. Track customer orders
  6. Start your own pickup service

Certification

Short courses, CAC business registration, and a record of reliable service.
