Cake Making & Decoration

Design and decorate birthday, wedding and celebration cakes with buttercream and fondant.

Your first step

Practise piping 10 different patterns on a plate with buttercream before you decorate your first cake.

Summary of the work

Design and decorate birthday, wedding and celebration cakes with buttercream and fondant.

Skills training

  • Cake baking and levelling
  • Buttercream piping
  • Fondant covering and modelling
  • Tiered cake structure
  • Pricing and delivery

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepPractise piping 10 different patterns on a plate with buttercream before you decorate your first cake.
  2. Bake and level sponge cakes
  3. Practise buttercream piping
  4. Cover cakes with fondant
  5. Build a tiered cake with supervision
  6. Photograph and sell your designs

Certification

Food handler training and NAFDAC registration for packaged products; trade test for catering where offered.
