Event Decoration & Planning

Summary of the work

Decorate and plan weddings, birthdays and corporate events with balloons, flowers, drapes and lighting.

Skills training

  • Balloon and floral decor
  • Draping and table setting
  • Event planning and timelines
  • Vendor management
  • Budgeting

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVolunteer to help a decorator set up for one event and take photos of each stage.
  2. Learn decor materials
  3. Assist a decorator at events

  6. Run your own events
