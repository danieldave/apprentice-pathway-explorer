Snail Farming

Raise giant African land snails in pens for meat and sale, with low start-up costs.

Quick answer: to learn snail farming in Nigeria, train for about 1–3 months under a master or at a skills centre. Build one small wooden snail pen and stock it with a few healthy snails to learn their routine.

Your first step

Build one small wooden snail pen and stock it with a few healthy snails to learn their routine.

Summary of the work

Raise giant African land snails in pens for meat and sale, with low start-up costs.

Skills training

  • Pen construction
  • Soil and humidity control
  • Feeding
  • Breeding and egg care
  • Harvesting and sales

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepBuild one small wooden snail pen and stock it with a few healthy snails to learn their routine.
  2. Learn snail biology and pens
  3. Build a small pen
  4. Stock and feed snails
  5. Manage breeding
  6. Harvest and sell

Certification

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.

Questions people ask

How long does it take to learn snail farming in Nigeria?

Training usually takes 1–3 months. The path: Learn snail biology and pens, then Build a small pen, then Stock and feed snails, then Manage breeding, then Harvest and sell.

How do I start learning snail farming?

Build one small wooden snail pen and stock it with a few healthy snails to learn their routine.

What skills will I learn in snail farming?

Pen construction, Soil and humidity control, Feeding, Breeding and egg care, Harvesting and sales.

What certificate do I need for snail farming?

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.

How much does it cost to start snail farming?

Low start-up cost compared with other trades. We do not list exact prices because they change often: ask two or three masters, compare, and agree the training fee in writing before you start.

Where can I learn snail farming in Nigeria?

Most people learn under a master (oga) in a workshop, at a state or church/mosque skills acquisition centre, or at a government technical college. National programmes such as the ITF Skill-Up Artisans (SUPA) programme and the federal TVET initiative also train and certify people; confirm details on the official .gov.ng website.
