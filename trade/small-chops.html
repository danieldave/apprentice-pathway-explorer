Small Chops & Pastry

Make puff-puff, samosa, spring rolls, chin-chin and party packs for events and offices.

Your first step

Make one batch of puff-puff this week, then sell or share it and ask for honest feedback.

Summary of the work

Make puff-puff, samosa, spring rolls, chin-chin and party packs for events and offices.

Skills training

  • Dough and batter making
  • Safe deep frying
  • Fillings and spices
  • Packing party trays
  • Pricing per pack

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepMake one batch of puff-puff this week, then sell or share it and ask for honest feedback.
  2. Learn hygiene and frying safety
  3. Make puff-puff and chin-chin
  4. Make samosa and spring rolls
  5. Pack trays for an event
  6. Take your first paid order

Certification

Food handler training and NAFDAC registration for packaged products; trade test for catering where offered.
