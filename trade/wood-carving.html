Wood Carving & Sculpture

Carve masks, figures, doors, panels and decor from wood for homes, hotels and collectors.

Your first step

Carve a simple spoon from soft wood to learn how the grain behaves.

Summary of the work

Carve masks, figures, doors, panels and decor from wood for homes, hotels and collectors.

Skills training

  • Choosing and preparing wood
  • Chisel and mallet work
  • Detailed carving
  • Sanding and finishing
  • Pricing art

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepCarve a simple spoon from soft wood to learn how the grain behaves.
  2. Learn wood types and tools
  3. Carve simple shapes
  4. Carve a small figure with supervision
  5. Finish and polish
  6. Exhibit and sell

Certification

Portfolio, exhibitions and craft fairs; trade test where offered.
