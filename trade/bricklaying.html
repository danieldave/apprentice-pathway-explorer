Bricklaying & Block Laying

Build walls, fences, columns and foundations with blocks and mortar. Every house in Nigeria starts with a mason.

Your first step

Visit a building site near you and ask the mason in charge if you can assist for one week: carry blocks, mix mortar and watch how corners are set.

Summary of the work

Build walls, fences, columns and foundations with blocks and mortar. Every house in Nigeria starts with a mason.

Skills training

  • Setting out and levelling with line and spirit level
  • Mixing mortar to the right ratio
  • Laying and bonding blocks
  • Reading simple building plans
  • Estimating blocks, sand and cement

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit a building site near you and ask the mason in charge if you can assist for one week: carry blocks, mix mortar and watch how corners are set.
  2. Learn tools, materials and site safety
  3. Mix mortar and lay blocks to a line
  4. Build corners, pillars and lintels with supervision
  5. Read plans and set out a building
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Take contracts and lead a small crew

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) through the Federal Ministry of Labour or your state examinations board; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate.
