Tailoring (Native & Suits)

Sew agbada, kaftan, senator, buba and sokoto, and suits that fit perfectly.

Your first step

Learn to take 10 body measurements correctly on a family member, and practise straight lines on a sewing machine.

Summary of the work

Sew agbada, kaftan, senator, buba and sokoto, and suits that fit perfectly.

Skills training

  • Taking accurate measurements
  • Pattern drafting and cutting
  • Machine and hand sewing
  • Embroidery placement
  • Customer fittings

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepLearn to take 10 body measurements correctly on a family member, and practise straight lines on a sewing machine.
  2. Learn measurements and the machine
  3. Sew straight seams and simple pieces
  4. Draft patterns and cut fabric
  5. Sew full outfits with supervision
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Receive your freedom and open a shop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.
