Goldsmithing & Jewellery

Make and repair rings, chains and earrings in gold, silver and brass.

Your first step

Make a simple copper wire ring to learn bending, filing and polishing.

Summary of the work

Make and repair rings, chains and earrings in gold, silver and brass.

Skills training

  • Metal cutting and shaping
  • Soldering
  • Stone setting
  • Polishing
  • Weighing and pricing metals

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepMake a simple copper wire ring to learn bending, filing and polishing.
  2. Learn metals and safety
  3. Make simple rings in brass
  4. Solder and repair chains
  5. Set stones with supervision
  6. Open your own jewellery bench

Certification

Portfolio, exhibitions and craft fairs; trade test where offered.
