POS & Mobile Money Agent

Run a POS and mobile money point for cash withdrawals, transfers and bill payments in your community.

Your first step

Work at a busy POS stand for two weeks and learn how they keep cash and records balanced every day.

Summary of the work

Run a POS and mobile money point for cash withdrawals, transfers and bill payments in your community.

Skills training

  • Using POS terminals
  • Cash handling and float management
  • Fraud and scam awareness
  • Record keeping
  • Customer service

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepWork at a busy POS stand for two weeks and learn how they keep cash and records balanced every day.
  2. Learn how agent banking works
  3. Work at an existing POS stand
  4. Register as an agent with a licensed provider
  5. Manage cash and records
  6. Grow your customer base

Certification

Short courses, CAC business registration, and a record of reliable service.
