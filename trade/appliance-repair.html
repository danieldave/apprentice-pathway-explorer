Your first step

Take apart a broken standing fan, find out why it stopped, and ask a repairer to check your answer.

Summary of the work

Fix fans, blenders, irons, microwaves and washing machines that every household depends on.

Skills training

  • Electrical safety
  • Motor and switch repair
  • Heating element repair
  • Spare parts sourcing
  • Fair pricing for repairs

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepTake apart a broken standing fan, find out why it stopped, and ask a repairer to check your answer.
  2. Learn appliance parts and safety
  3. Repair fans and irons
  4. Repair blenders and microwaves
  5. Handle washing machines with supervision
  6. Run your own repair corner

Certification

Trade test where offered; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; a strong record of repaired devices and repeat customers.
