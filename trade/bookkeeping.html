Bookkeeping & Small Business Accounts

Keep sales, expenses and stock records for shops and small businesses so they know if they are making profit.

Your first step

Record every sale and expense of a small shop you know for one week in a notebook.

Summary of the work

Keep sales, expenses and stock records for shops and small businesses so they know if they are making profit.

Skills training

  • Recording sales and expenses
  • Stock records
  • Excel and accounting apps
  • Profit and loss basics
  • Tax basics for small businesses

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepRecord every sale and expense of a small shop you know for one week in a notebook.
  2. Learn bookkeeping basics
  3. Keep records for a real shop
  4. Use an accounting app
  5. Prepare a monthly profit report
  6. Offer bookkeeping to several businesses

Certification

Short courses, CAC business registration, and a record of reliable service.
