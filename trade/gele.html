Gele & Head-tie Styling

Tie stunning gele and head-ties for weddings, owambe parties and photo shoots, and make auto-gele.

Your first step

Practise one classic gele style every day for a week until you can tie it in under five minutes.

Summary of the work

Tie stunning gele and head-ties for weddings, owambe parties and photo shoots, and make auto-gele.

Skills training

  • Fabric choice and folding
  • Classic and modern gele styles
  • Auto-gele making
  • Working fast at events
  • Social media marketing

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepPractise one classic gele style every day for a week until you can tie it in under five minutes.
  2. Learn fabrics and basic folds
  3. Practise three classic styles
  4. Tie for guests at an event with supervision
  5. Make auto-gele to sell
  6. Post your work and take bookings

Certification

Trade test for hairdressing where offered; portfolio, reviews and hygiene standards; NAFDAC registration for cosmetics you sell.
