Generator Repair

Service and repair petrol and diesel generators, from small home sets to large standby units.

Your first step

Spend a week at a generator repairer’s shop and learn to strip and clean a carburettor.

Summary of the work

Service and repair petrol and diesel generators, from small home sets to large standby units.

Skills training

  • Engine servicing
  • Carburettor and fuel system repair
  • Alternator and AVR faults
  • Starting system repair
  • Customer service

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepSpend a week at a generator repairer’s shop and learn to strip and clean a carburettor.
  2. Learn engine parts and safety
  3. Service small generators
  4. Repair fuel and starting faults
  5. Diagnose alternator problems
  6. Handle large diesel sets with supervision
  7. Open your own repair shop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; manufacturer training for solar and inverter brands.
