Heavy-Duty Truck Mechanic

Repair trucks, trailers and buses: diesel engines, air brakes, gearboxes and hydraulics.

Your first step

Visit a truck park or haulage company workshop and ask the head mechanic if they take apprentices.

Summary of the work

Repair trucks, trailers and buses: diesel engines, air brakes, gearboxes and hydraulics.

Skills training

  • Diesel engine repair
  • Air brake systems
  • Heavy gearbox and differential
  • Hydraulics
  • Workshop safety with heavy loads

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit a truck park or haulage company workshop and ask the head mechanic if they take apprentices.
  2. Learn diesel basics and safety
  3. Service trucks with your master
  4. Repair brakes and suspension
  5. Overhaul a diesel engine with supervision
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Work for fleets or open a workshop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.
