Vulcanizing & Wheel Balancing

Repair punctures, fit tyres and balance wheels. A fast trade to learn with daily cash income.

Your first step

Stand with a vulcanizer for a busy morning and count how many jobs come in. That tells you how quickly you can earn.

Summary of the work

Repair punctures, fit tyres and balance wheels. A fast trade to learn with daily cash income.

Skills training

  • Puncture repair and patching
  • Tyre fitting
  • Wheel balancing
  • Tyre pressure and safety checks
  • Compressor care

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepStand with a vulcanizer for a busy morning and count how many jobs come in. That tells you how quickly you can earn.
  2. Learn tools and tyre safety
  3. Repair punctures
  4. Fit tyres with supervision
  5. Balance wheels
  6. Set up your own stand

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.
