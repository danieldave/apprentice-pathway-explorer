Fashion Design & Dressmaking

Design and make dresses, aso-ebi styles and ready-to-wear collections with your own label.

Your first step

Sketch five outfits you would love to make, then ask a fashion designer which one a beginner could sew first.

Summary of the work

Design and make dresses, aso-ebi styles and ready-to-wear collections with your own label.

Skills training

  • Fashion illustration
  • Pattern drafting
  • Draping and fitting
  • Finishing and quality
  • Branding and social media sales

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepSketch five outfits you would love to make, then ask a fashion designer which one a beginner could sew first.
  2. Learn sewing basics and measurements
  3. Draft patterns for simple styles
  4. Make full dresses with supervision
  5. Design a small collection
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Launch your own label

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.
