Professional Driving

Drive for companies, families, logistics firms and ride-hailing apps safely and professionally.

Your first step

Find an FRSC-accredited driving school near you and ask for their course length, fees and schedule.

Summary of the work

Drive for companies, families, logistics firms and ride-hailing apps safely and professionally.

Skills training

  • Defensive driving
  • Road signs and traffic rules
  • Basic vehicle checks
  • Map and route planning
  • Customer care

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepFind an FRSC-accredited driving school near you and ask for their course length, fees and schedule.
  2. Enrol at an accredited driving school
  3. Learn road signs and rules
  4. Practise driving with an instructor
  5. Get your FRSC driver’s licence
  6. Apply to companies or driving apps

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.
