Palm Oil Processing

Process palm fruit into clean red oil and palm kernel products for homes and industry.

Your first step

Visit a small palm oil mill in harvest season and watch a full processing day.

Summary of the work

Process palm fruit into clean red oil and palm kernel products for homes and industry.

Skills training

  • Fruit sorting and boiling
  • Digester and press operation
  • Oil clarification
  • Storage and quality
  • Bulk sales

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit a small palm oil mill in harvest season and watch a full processing day.
  2. Learn the processing steps
  3. Assist at a palm oil mill
  4. Operate the press with supervision
  5. Clarify and store oil
  6. Sell in bulk

Certification

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.
