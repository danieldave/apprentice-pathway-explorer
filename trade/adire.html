Adire & Tie-Dye

Create traditional and modern dyed fabrics using tie, stitch and wax-resist methods.

Quick answer: to learn adire & tie-dye in Nigeria, train for about 2–6 months under a master or at a skills centre. Tie-dye one white T-shirt at home with a simple dye kit to learn how patterns form.

Your first step

Tie-dye one white T-shirt at home with a simple dye kit to learn how patterns form.

Summary of the work

Create traditional and modern dyed fabrics using tie, stitch and wax-resist methods.

Known clusters: Itoku (Abeokuta, Ogun State) and Kofar Mata dye pits (Kano).

Skills training

  • Fabric preparation
  • Tying and stitching patterns
  • Wax and paste resist
  • Dye mixing and fixing
  • Selling fabric and finished items

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepTie-dye one white T-shirt at home with a simple dye kit to learn how patterns form.
  2. Learn fabrics and dye safety
  3. Make simple tie-dye patterns
  4. Learn wax and stitch resist
  5. Produce a batch with supervision
  6. Brand and sell your fabrics

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.

Questions people ask

How long does it take to learn adire & tie-dye in Nigeria?

Training usually takes 2–6 months. The path: Learn fabrics and dye safety, then Make simple tie-dye patterns, then Learn wax and stitch resist, then Produce a batch with supervision, then Brand and sell your fabrics.

How do I start learning adire & tie-dye?

Tie-dye one white T-shirt at home with a simple dye kit to learn how patterns form.

What skills will I learn in adire & tie-dye?

Fabric preparation, Tying and stitching patterns, Wax and paste resist, Dye mixing and fixing, Selling fabric and finished items.

What certificate do I need for adire & tie-dye?

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.

How much does it cost to start adire & tie-dye?

Low start-up cost compared with other trades. We do not list exact prices because they change often: ask two or three masters, compare, and agree the training fee in writing before you start.

Where can I learn adire & tie-dye in Nigeria?

Most people learn under a master (oga) in a workshop, at a state or church/mosque skills acquisition centre, or at a government technical college. Known clusters: Itoku (Abeokuta, Ogun State) and Kofar Mata dye pits (Kano). National programmes such as the ITF Skill-Up Artisans (SUPA) programme and the federal TVET initiative also train and certify people; confirm details on the official .gov.ng website.
