Adire & Tie-Dye

Create traditional and modern dyed fabrics using tie, stitch and wax-resist methods.

Your first step

Tie-dye one white T-shirt at home with a simple dye kit to learn how patterns form.

Summary of the work

Create traditional and modern dyed fabrics using tie, stitch and wax-resist methods.

Known clusters: Itoku (Abeokuta, Ogun State) and Kofar Mata dye pits (Kano).

Skills training

  • Fabric preparation
  • Tying and stitching patterns
  • Wax and paste resist
  • Dye mixing and fixing
  • Selling fabric and finished items

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepTie-dye one white T-shirt at home with a simple dye kit to learn how patterns form.
  2. Learn fabrics and dye safety
  3. Make simple tie-dye patterns
  4. Learn wax and stitch resist
  5. Produce a batch with supervision
  6. Brand and sell your fabrics

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.
