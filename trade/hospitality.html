Hotel & Hospitality Services

Work front desk, housekeeping, restaurant service and events in hotels, lounges and resorts.

Your first step

Ask a hotel near you about trainee or casual staff roles, and practise greeting and serving guests politely.

Summary of the work

Work front desk, housekeeping, restaurant service and events in hotels, lounges and resorts.

Skills training

  • Guest service and etiquette
  • Housekeeping standards
  • Restaurant and bar service
  • Front-desk systems
  • Handling complaints calmly

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepAsk a hotel near you about trainee or casual staff roles, and practise greeting and serving guests politely.
  2. Learn hospitality basics
  3. Train in housekeeping
  4. Train in restaurant service
  5. Work front desk with supervision
  6. Apply for hotel roles

Certification

Food handler training and NAFDAC registration for packaged products; trade test for catering where offered.
