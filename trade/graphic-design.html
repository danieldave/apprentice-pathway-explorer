Graphic Design

Design logos, flyers, social posts and packaging that make brands look professional.

Your first step

Redesign one flyer you see in your area and ask a designer for feedback.

Summary of the work

Design logos, flyers, social posts and packaging that make brands look professional.

Skills training

  • Layout and typography
  • Colour and branding
  • Photoshop, Illustrator, CorelDRAW or Canva
  • Print preparation
  • Working with clients

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepRedesign one flyer you see in your area and ask a designer for feedback.
  2. Learn design principles
  3. Learn one design tool well
  4. Redesign real flyers
  5. Design for small businesses with supervision
  6. Build a portfolio
  7. Take paying clients

Certification

A strong portfolio and client reviews; short courses and industry certificates.
