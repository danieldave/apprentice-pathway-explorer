Machining (Lathe & Milling)

Make and repair precise machine parts like shafts, bushings and gears on lathe and milling machines.

Your first step

Learn to read a vernier caliper accurately, then visit a machine shop and ask to watch a shaft being turned.

Summary of the work

Make and repair precise machine parts like shafts, bushings and gears on lathe and milling machines.

Skills training

  • Lathe operation
  • Milling and drilling
  • Precision measuring (vernier, micrometer)
  • Reading engineering drawings
  • Machine maintenance

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepLearn to read a vernier caliper accurately, then visit a machine shop and ask to watch a shaft being turned.
  2. Learn machine safety and measuring tools
  3. Turn simple parts on a lathe
  4. Mill and drill to drawings
  5. Make spare parts with supervision
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Work in industry or open a machine shop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; welding codes for industrial jobs.
