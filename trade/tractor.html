Tractor Operation & Farm Mechanisation

Operate and maintain tractors and farm machines for ploughing, planting and harvesting services.

Your first step

Contact your state Agricultural Development Programme (ADP) or a farm mechanisation centre about operator training.

Summary of the work

Operate and maintain tractors and farm machines for ploughing, planting and harvesting services.

Skills training

  • Tractor controls and safety
  • Ploughing and harrowing
  • Implement hitching
  • Daily maintenance
  • Running hire services

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepContact your state Agricultural Development Programme (ADP) or a farm mechanisation centre about operator training.
  2. Learn tractor parts and safety
  3. Practise driving in an open field
  4. Plough with supervision
  5. Maintain the tractor and implements
  6. Offer hire services to farmers

Certification

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.
