Beekeeping

Keep bees for honey and beeswax, and help farms with pollination.

Quick answer: to learn beekeeping in Nigeria, train for about 3–6 months under a master or at a skills centre. Join a beekeeper on a harvest day, wearing full protective gear.

Your first step

Join a beekeeper on a harvest day, wearing full protective gear.

Summary of the work

Keep bees for honey and beeswax, and help farms with pollination.

Skills training

  • Hive building and siting
  • Safe hive inspection
  • Harvesting honey
  • Processing and bottling
  • Selling honey and wax

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepJoin a beekeeper on a harvest day, wearing full protective gear.
  2. Learn bee behaviour and safety
  3. Build or buy a hive
  4. Inspect hives with a beekeeper
  5. Harvest and bottle honey
  6. Sell and expand your apiary

Certification

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.

Questions people ask

How long does it take to learn beekeeping in Nigeria?

Training usually takes 3–6 months. The path: Learn bee behaviour and safety, then Build or buy a hive, then Inspect hives with a beekeeper, then Harvest and bottle honey, then Sell and expand your apiary.

How do I start learning beekeeping?

Join a beekeeper on a harvest day, wearing full protective gear.

What skills will I learn in beekeeping?

Hive building and siting, Safe hive inspection, Harvesting honey, Processing and bottling, Selling honey and wax.

What certificate do I need for beekeeping?

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.

How much does it cost to start beekeeping?

Low start-up cost compared with other trades. We do not list exact prices because they change often: ask two or three masters, compare, and agree the training fee in writing before you start.

Where can I learn beekeeping in Nigeria?

Most people learn under a master (oga) in a workshop, at a state or church/mosque skills acquisition centre, or at a government technical college. National programmes such as the ITF Skill-Up Artisans (SUPA) programme and the federal TVET initiative also train and certify people; confirm details on the official .gov.ng website.
