Electrical Installation (House Wiring)

Wire homes, shops and offices, install fittings and distribution boards, and find faults safely.

Your first step

Buy or borrow a multimeter and learn to measure voltage and continuity safely, then ask an electrician to let you assist on a house wiring job.

Summary of the work

Wire homes, shops and offices, install fittings and distribution boards, and find faults safely.

Skills training

  • Conduit and surface wiring
  • Installing sockets, switches and fittings
  • Distribution boards and breakers
  • Fault finding with a multimeter
  • Earthing and electrical safety

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepBuy or borrow a multimeter and learn to measure voltage and continuity safely, then ask an electrician to let you assist on a house wiring job.
  2. Learn safety, tools and cable sizes
  3. Wire practice boards
  4. Wire rooms with supervision
  5. Install distribution boards and find faults
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Take wiring contracts

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; manufacturer training for solar and inverter brands.
