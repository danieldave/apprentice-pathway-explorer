Satellite Dish & TV Installation

Install and align satellite dishes, decoders and TV mounts for homes, hotels and shops.

Your first step

Ask a dish installer if you can carry the ladder and tools for a week while you learn how they find the signal.

Summary of the work

Install and align satellite dishes, decoders and TV mounts for homes, hotels and shops.

Skills training

  • Dish assembly and mounting
  • Signal alignment
  • Decoder setup
  • Wall-mounting TVs
  • Cable routing

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepAsk a dish installer if you can carry the ladder and tools for a week while you learn how they find the signal.
  2. Learn dish parts and tools
  3. Assist on installations
  4. Align a dish with a signal meter
  5. Mount TVs safely
  6. Build a customer list

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; manufacturer training for solar and inverter brands.
