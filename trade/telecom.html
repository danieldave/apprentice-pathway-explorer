Telecom Mast Technician

Install and maintain telecom masts, antennas, fibre and power systems that keep networks on.

Your first step

Complete a working-at-height safety course before anything else. Contractors ask for it first.

Summary of the work

Install and maintain telecom masts, antennas, fibre and power systems that keep networks on.

Skills training

  • Working at height safely
  • Antenna and radio installation
  • Fibre splicing
  • Site power and generators
  • Fault reporting

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepComplete a working-at-height safety course before anything else. Contractors ask for it first.
  2. Get height safety training
  3. Learn site equipment
  4. Assist a maintenance team
  5. Splice fibre and install antennas
  6. Work for a telecom contractor

Certification

NABTEB or polytechnic certificates; company and safety certifications; professional registration where the law requires it.
