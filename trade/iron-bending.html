Iron Bending (Steel Fixing)

Cut, bend and tie reinforcement rods for columns, beams and slabs that hold buildings up.

Your first step

Visit a site where a slab is being prepared and ask the iron bender in charge to let you help tie stirrups for a few days.

Summary of the work

Cut, bend and tie reinforcement rods for columns, beams and slabs that hold buildings up.

Skills training

  • Reading structural drawings
  • Cutting and bending rods
  • Tying stirrups and mesh
  • Placing spacers and cover
  • Estimating rods and binding wire

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit a site where a slab is being prepared and ask the iron bender in charge to let you help tie stirrups for a few days.
  2. Learn rod sizes and site safety
  3. Cut and bend rods to measurement
  4. Tie columns and beams with supervision
  5. Read drawings and set out slabs
  6. Lead a section of a site
  7. Take sub-contracts on your own

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) through the Federal Ministry of Labour or your state examinations board; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate.
