Phone Repair

Replace screens, batteries and charging ports, and fix software and board faults on phones and tablets.

Your first step

Get an old broken phone, open it carefully with the right tools, and learn the name of every part inside.

Summary of the work

Replace screens, batteries and charging ports, and fix software and board faults on phones and tablets.

Known clusters: Computer Village, Ikeja (Lagos) and phone markets in every state capital.

Skills training

  • Opening devices safely
  • Screen, battery and port replacement
  • Soldering and micro-soldering
  • Software flashing and unlocking legally
  • Customer service at the counter

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepGet an old broken phone, open it carefully with the right tools, and learn the name of every part inside.
  2. Learn phone parts and tools
  3. Replace screens and batteries
  4. Learn soldering on scrap boards
  5. Do board-level repairs
  6. Handle customers at the counter
  7. Open your own repair stand

Certification

Trade test where offered; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; a strong record of repaired devices and repeat customers.
