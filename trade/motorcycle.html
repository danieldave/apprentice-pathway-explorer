Motorcycle & Keke Repair

Service and repair motorcycles and tricycles that move people and goods across Nigeria.

Your first step

Visit a keke or okada park, find the mechanic everyone uses, and ask to learn under them.

Summary of the work

Service and repair motorcycles and tricycles that move people and goods across Nigeria.

Skills training

  • Engine servicing
  • Clutch, chain and gear repair
  • Carburettor tuning
  • Electrical and lighting faults
  • Brake repair

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit a keke or okada park, find the mechanic everyone uses, and ask to learn under them.
  2. Learn engine parts and tools
  3. Service bikes and keke
  4. Repair clutch and chain systems
  5. Overhaul an engine with supervision
  6. Build a customer base at a park
  7. Open your own repair shop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.
