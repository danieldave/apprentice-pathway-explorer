Logistics & Dispatch Operations

Run deliveries for online shops, restaurants and companies: routes, riders, packages and payments.

Your first step

Learn the main routes in your city on a map app and shadow a dispatch rider for a few days.

Summary of the work

Run deliveries for online shops, restaurants and companies: routes, riders, packages and payments.

Skills training

  • Route planning
  • Safe riding and driving
  • Package handling
  • Delivery apps and tracking
  • Customer service

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepLearn the main routes in your city on a map app and shadow a dispatch rider for a few days.
  2. Learn safety and routes
  3. Ride with an experienced dispatcher
  4. Handle deliveries and payments
  5. Coordinate several riders
  6. Start your own dispatch service

Certification

Short courses, CAC business registration, and a record of reliable service.
