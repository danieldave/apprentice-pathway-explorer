Soap, Cream & Cosmetics Production

Make liquid soap, bar soap, body creams, shea butter blends and hair products safely.

Your first step

Join a hands-on soap-making class and make your first batch of liquid soap with a trainer present.

Summary of the work

Make liquid soap, bar soap, body creams, shea butter blends and hair products safely.

Skills training

  • Ingredient safety
  • Liquid and bar soap making
  • Creams and butters
  • Packaging and labelling
  • NAFDAC registration steps

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepJoin a hands-on soap-making class and make your first batch of liquid soap with a trainer present.
  2. Learn safe handling of chemicals
  3. Make liquid soap
  4. Make creams and butters
  5. Package and label
  6. Register and sell

Certification

Trade test for hairdressing where offered; portfolio, reviews and hygiene standards; NAFDAC registration for cosmetics you sell.
