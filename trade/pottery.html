Pottery & Ceramics

Shape clay into pots, vases and decor on the wheel or by hand, then fire and glaze them.

Your first step

Visit a pottery and make one simple pinch pot with your hands.

Summary of the work

Shape clay into pots, vases and decor on the wheel or by hand, then fire and glaze them.

Known clusters: Ilorin (Kwara State) and the Ladi Kwali pottery centre, Suleja (Niger State).

Skills training

  • Clay preparation
  • Wheel throwing
  • Hand building
  • Firing and glazing
  • Selling to homes, hotels and tourists

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit a pottery and make one simple pinch pot with your hands.
  2. Learn clay and kiln safety
  3. Hand-build simple pots
  4. Throw on the wheel
  5. Fire and glaze with supervision
  6. Sell at fairs and online

Certification

Portfolio, exhibitions and craft fairs; trade test where offered.
