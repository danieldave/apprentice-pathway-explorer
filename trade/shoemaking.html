Shoemaking & Leatherwork

Make shoes, sandals, belts and leather goods by hand and with machines.

Your first step

Make one pair of simple leather slippers with a shoemaker’s guidance.

Summary of the work

Make shoes, sandals, belts and leather goods by hand and with machines.

Known clusters: Aba (Abia State) and leatherworking communities in Kano.

Skills training

  • Pattern cutting
  • Lasting and sole attachment
  • Leather stitching
  • Finishing and polishing
  • Bulk production for markets

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepMake one pair of simple leather slippers with a shoemaker’s guidance.
  2. Learn leather types and tools
  3. Cut patterns and make slippers
  4. Make sandals and shoes with supervision
  5. Finish and package
  6. Produce for markets or your own brand

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.
