Goat, Sheep & Pig Rearing

Raise goats, sheep, pigs or rabbits for meat and breeding, with healthy housing and feeding.

Your first step

Spend two weeks on a livestock farm learning daily feeding and cleaning routines.

Summary of the work

Raise goats, sheep, pigs or rabbits for meat and breeding, with healthy housing and feeding.

Skills training

  • Housing and hygiene
  • Feeding and forage
  • Breeding management
  • Common diseases and vet care
  • Selling at markets and festivals

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepSpend two weeks on a livestock farm learning daily feeding and cleaning routines.
  2. Learn animal care basics
  3. Work on a livestock farm
  4. Manage feeding and health
  5. Start with a few animals
  6. Breed, sell and grow your herd

Certification

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.
