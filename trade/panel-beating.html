Panel Beating & Spray Painting

Repair dents and accident damage on vehicles, then spray and polish them to look new.

Quick answer: to learn panel beating & spray painting in Nigeria, train for about 2–3 years under a master or at a skills centre. Spend a week at a panel beater’s workshop and learn how filler is mixed, applied and sanded smooth.

Your first step

Spend a week at a panel beater’s workshop and learn how filler is mixed, applied and sanded smooth.

Summary of the work

Repair dents and accident damage on vehicles, then spray and polish them to look new.

Skills training

  • Dent removal and body filling
  • Welding body panels
  • Surface preparation and masking
  • Colour mixing and spraying
  • Buffing and polishing

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepSpend a week at a panel beater’s workshop and learn how filler is mixed, applied and sanded smooth.
  2. Learn tools, fillers and safety gear
  3. Beat out dents on practice panels
  4. Prepare and mask a car
  5. Spray with supervision
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Open your own body shop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.

Questions people ask

How long does it take to learn panel beating & spray painting in Nigeria?

Training usually takes 2–3 years. The path: Learn tools, fillers and safety gear, then Beat out dents on practice panels, then Prepare and mask a car, then Spray with supervision, then Sit a trade test (Grade III), then Open your own body shop.

How do I start learning panel beating & spray painting?

Spend a week at a panel beater’s workshop and learn how filler is mixed, applied and sanded smooth.

What skills will I learn in panel beating & spray painting?

Dent removal and body filling, Welding body panels, Surface preparation and masking, Colour mixing and spraying, Buffing and polishing.

What certificate do I need for panel beating & spray painting?

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.

How much does it cost to start panel beating & spray painting?

Medium start-up cost compared with other trades. We do not list exact prices because they change often: ask two or three masters, compare, and agree the training fee in writing before you start.

Where can I learn panel beating & spray painting in Nigeria?

Most people learn under a master (oga) in a workshop, at a state or church/mosque skills acquisition centre, or at a government technical college. National programmes such as the ITF Skill-Up Artisans (SUPA) programme and the federal TVET initiative also train and certify people; confirm details on the official .gov.ng website.
