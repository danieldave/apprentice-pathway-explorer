Panel Beating & Spray Painting

Repair dents and accident damage on vehicles, then spray and polish them to look new.

Your first step

Spend a week at a panel beater’s workshop and learn how filler is mixed, applied and sanded smooth.

Summary of the work

Repair dents and accident damage on vehicles, then spray and polish them to look new.

Skills training

  • Dent removal and body filling
  • Welding body panels
  • Surface preparation and masking
  • Colour mixing and spraying
  • Buffing and polishing

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepSpend a week at a panel beater’s workshop and learn how filler is mixed, applied and sanded smooth.
  2. Learn tools, fillers and safety gear
  3. Beat out dents on practice panels
  4. Prepare and mask a car
  5. Spray with supervision
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Open your own body shop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.
