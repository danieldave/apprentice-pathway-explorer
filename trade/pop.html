POP Ceiling & Screeding

Create smooth POP walls, decorative ceilings and cornices that give buildings a modern finish.

Your first step

Find a POP installer working on a site near you and offer to help mix and carry for a week, so you see a ceiling go up from start to finish.

Summary of the work

Create smooth POP walls, decorative ceilings and cornices that give buildings a modern finish.

Skills training

  • Mixing POP to the right consistency
  • Screeding and skimming walls
  • Casting and fixing ceiling boards
  • Making cornices and designs
  • Working safely on scaffolds

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepFind a POP installer working on a site near you and offer to help mix and carry for a week, so you see a ceiling go up from start to finish.
  2. Learn materials, tools and scaffold safety
  3. Mix POP and skim practice walls
  4. Screed full rooms with supervision
  5. Cast boards and fix a ceiling
  6. Design cornices and price jobs
  7. Run your own POP jobs

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) through the Federal Ministry of Labour or your state examinations board; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate.
