Aso-Oke Weaving

Weave aso-oke on traditional and broad looms for weddings, chieftaincy and festive wear.

Your first step

Visit an aso-oke weaver and watch how the loom is warped before a single strip is woven.

Summary of the work

Weave aso-oke on traditional and broad looms for weddings, chieftaincy and festive wear.

Known clusters: Iseyin (Oyo State) and weaving communities across the South-West.

Skills training

  • Setting up the loom
  • Warping and threading
  • Weaving patterns
  • Finishing and joining strips
  • Pricing custom orders

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit an aso-oke weaver and watch how the loom is warped before a single strip is woven.
  2. Learn yarns and the loom
  3. Warp and thread a loom
  4. Weave plain strips
  5. Weave patterns with supervision
  6. Take custom wedding orders

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.
