Aso-Oke Weaving

Weave aso-oke on traditional and broad looms for weddings, chieftaincy and festive wear.

Quick answer: to learn aso-oke weaving in Nigeria, train for about 1–2 years under a master or at a skills centre. Visit an aso-oke weaver and watch how the loom is warped before a single strip is woven.

Your first step

Visit an aso-oke weaver and watch how the loom is warped before a single strip is woven.

Summary of the work

Weave aso-oke on traditional and broad looms for weddings, chieftaincy and festive wear.

Known clusters: Iseyin (Oyo State) and weaving communities across the South-West.

Skills training

  • Setting up the loom
  • Warping and threading
  • Weaving patterns
  • Finishing and joining strips
  • Pricing custom orders

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit an aso-oke weaver and watch how the loom is warped before a single strip is woven.
  2. Learn yarns and the loom
  3. Warp and thread a loom
  4. Weave plain strips
  5. Weave patterns with supervision
  6. Take custom wedding orders

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.

Questions people ask

How long does it take to learn aso-oke weaving in Nigeria?

Training usually takes 1–2 years. The path: Learn yarns and the loom, then Warp and thread a loom, then Weave plain strips, then Weave patterns with supervision, then Take custom wedding orders.

How do I start learning aso-oke weaving?

Visit an aso-oke weaver and watch how the loom is warped before a single strip is woven.

What skills will I learn in aso-oke weaving?

Setting up the loom, Warping and threading, Weaving patterns, Finishing and joining strips, Pricing custom orders.

What certificate do I need for aso-oke weaving?

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.

How much does it cost to start aso-oke weaving?

Medium start-up cost compared with other trades. We do not list exact prices because they change often: ask two or three masters, compare, and agree the training fee in writing before you start.

Where can I learn aso-oke weaving in Nigeria?

Most people learn under a master (oga) in a workshop, at a state or church/mosque skills acquisition centre, or at a government technical college. Known clusters: Iseyin (Oyo State) and weaving communities across the South-West. National programmes such as the ITF Skill-Up Artisans (SUPA) programme and the federal TVET initiative also train and certify people; confirm details on the official .gov.ng website.
