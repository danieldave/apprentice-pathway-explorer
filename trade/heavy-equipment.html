Heavy Equipment Operation

Operate excavators, bulldozers and payloaders on road, mining and building projects.

Your first step

Look for an accredited heavy-equipment training school or a construction firm that trains operators, and ask about their next intake.

Summary of the work

Operate excavators, bulldozers and payloaders on road, mining and building projects.

Skills training

  • Pre-start equipment checks
  • Safe machine control
  • Digging, loading and grading
  • Site signals and teamwork
  • Basic machine maintenance

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepLook for an accredited heavy-equipment training school or a construction firm that trains operators, and ask about their next intake.
  2. Learn machine parts and site safety
  3. Practise controls on a training machine
  4. Operate under supervision on site
  5. Do daily checks and basic maintenance
  6. Get certified by a training centre
  7. Work for construction and mining firms

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) through the Federal Ministry of Labour or your state examinations board; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate.
