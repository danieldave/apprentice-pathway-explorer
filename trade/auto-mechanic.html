Auto Mechanic

Diagnose and repair cars, from engines, gearboxes and brakes to computer-controlled systems.

Your first step

Visit two mechanic workshops, watch for a full day at each, and choose the master who explains what they are doing.

Summary of the work

Diagnose and repair cars, from engines, gearboxes and brakes to computer-controlled systems.

Known clusters: Mechanic villages in every city; spare-parts clusters such as Ladipo (Lagos) and Nnewi (Anambra).

Skills training

  • Engine servicing and overhaul
  • Brakes, suspension and steering
  • Computer diagnostics (OBD scanner)
  • Gearbox and clutch repair
  • Honest customer communication

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepVisit two mechanic workshops, watch for a full day at each, and choose the master who explains what they are doing.
  2. Learn car parts, tools and workshop safety
  3. Service cars: oil, plugs, filters
  4. Repair brakes and suspension
  5. Diagnose engine faults with a scanner
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Receive your freedom and open a workshop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.
