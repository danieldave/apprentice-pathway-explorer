Plumbing & Pipe Fitting

Install pipes, tanks, pumps, toilets and water heaters, and fix leaks in homes and new buildings.

Your first step

Learn the names of 20 common plumbing fittings at a building materials shop, then ask a plumber to take you along to a repair job.

Summary of the work

Install pipes, tanks, pumps, toilets and water heaters, and fix leaks in homes and new buildings.

Skills training

  • Cutting and joining PVC and PPR pipes
  • Fitting sanitary wares
  • Installing pumps and overhead tanks
  • Leak detection and repair
  • Reading plumbing layouts

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepLearn the names of 20 common plumbing fittings at a building materials shop, then ask a plumber to take you along to a repair job.
  2. Learn pipes, fittings and tools
  3. Assist on site and fix simple leaks
  4. Fit a bathroom with supervision
  5. Install pumps and tank systems
  6. Sit a trade test (Grade III)
  7. Take your own plumbing jobs

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) through the Federal Ministry of Labour or your state examinations board; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate.
