Poultry Farming

Raise broilers and layers for meat and eggs, from day-old chicks to market.

Your first step

Work on an existing poultry farm for one full batch cycle before you buy your own birds.

Summary of the work

Raise broilers and layers for meat and eggs, from day-old chicks to market.

Skills training

  • Brooding day-old chicks
  • Feeding and water management
  • Vaccination schedules
  • Disease prevention and biosecurity
  • Record keeping and sales

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepWork on an existing poultry farm for one full batch cycle before you buy your own birds.
  2. Learn poultry housing and biosecurity
  3. Brood chicks on a working farm
  4. Follow a vaccination schedule
  5. Raise a small batch
  6. Keep records and sell
  7. Scale your own farm

Certification

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.
