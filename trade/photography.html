Your first step

Take 50 photos with your phone this week using window light, and pick your best five.

Summary of the work

Capture weddings, events, products and portraits, and edit them into photos people pay for.

Skills training

  • Camera settings and lighting
  • Posing and directing people
  • Photo editing
  • Event coverage
  • Pricing and booking

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepTake 50 photos with your phone this week using window light, and pick your best five.
  2. Learn exposure and light
  3. Assist at events
  4. Shoot and edit on your own
  5. Cover an event with supervision
  6. Build a portfolio
  7. Run your own studio or brand

Certification

A strong portfolio and client reviews; short courses and industry certificates.
