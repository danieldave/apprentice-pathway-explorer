Electric Motor Rewinding

Rewind and repair electric motors, pumps, fans and transformers instead of throwing them away.

Your first step

Find a rewinder near a market or industrial area and ask to watch them strip and rewind one pumping machine.

Summary of the work

Rewind and repair electric motors, pumps, fans and transformers instead of throwing them away.

Skills training

  • Motor and coil theory
  • Stripping and recording windings
  • Rewinding and insulating coils
  • Testing and varnishing
  • Bearing replacement

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepFind a rewinder near a market or industrial area and ask to watch them strip and rewind one pumping machine.
  2. Learn motor types and tools
  3. Strip and record old windings
  4. Rewind small motors with supervision
  5. Test, varnish and assemble
  6. Repair pumps and industrial motors
  7. Run your own rewinding shop

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; manufacturer training for solar and inverter brands.
