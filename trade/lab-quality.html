Laboratory & Food Quality Control

Test and inspect food, water and products in factories and labs so they are safe and consistent.

Your first step

Learn the names and uses of 15 basic lab instruments, then seek an industrial attachment in a food factory.

Summary of the work

Test and inspect food, water and products in factories and labs so they are safe and consistent.

Skills training

  • Lab safety
  • Sampling and testing
  • Hygiene standards
  • Record keeping
  • Process inspection

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepLearn the names and uses of 15 basic lab instruments, then seek an industrial attachment in a food factory.
  2. Learn lab safety and equipment
  3. Assist with tests
  4. Inspect a production line
  5. Write quality reports
  6. Take charge of a quality checkpoint

Certification

NABTEB or polytechnic certificates; company and safety certifications; professional registration where the law requires it.
