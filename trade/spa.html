Spa & Massage Therapy

Offer massages, facials and body treatments in spas, hotels and gyms.

Your first step

Enrol in a short spa therapy course with practical sessions supervised by a certified therapist.

Summary of the work

Offer massages, facials and body treatments in spas, hotels and gyms.

Skills training

  • Anatomy basics
  • Massage techniques
  • Facials and skin care
  • Hygiene and client comfort
  • Booking and customer care

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepEnrol in a short spa therapy course with practical sessions supervised by a certified therapist.
  2. Learn anatomy and hygiene
  3. Practise massage techniques
  4. Do facials with supervision
  5. Work in a spa
  6. Start a mobile or home spa

Certification

Trade test for hairdressing where offered; portfolio, reviews and hygiene standards; NAFDAC registration for cosmetics you sell.
