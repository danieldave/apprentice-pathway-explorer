Vegetable & Greenhouse Farming

Grow tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers and leafy vegetables in open fields and greenhouses all year round.

Your first step

Plant a small bed of ugwu or tomatoes at home and keep a weekly growth diary.

Summary of the work

Grow tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers and leafy vegetables in open fields and greenhouses all year round.

Skills training

  • Nursery and transplanting
  • Drip irrigation
  • Soil and fertiliser management
  • Pest and disease control
  • Harvesting and market links

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepPlant a small bed of ugwu or tomatoes at home and keep a weekly growth diary.
  2. Learn soil, seeds and tools
  3. Raise a nursery
  4. Grow a plot with supervision
  5. Manage pests and irrigation
  6. Harvest and sell
  7. Expand or build a greenhouse

Certification

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.
