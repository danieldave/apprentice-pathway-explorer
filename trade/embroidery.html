Embroidery & Monogramming

Add machine and hand embroidery to kaftans, uniforms, caps and corporate wear.

Your first step

Practise five hand embroidery stitches on scrap fabric to train your eye and hands.

Summary of the work

Add machine and hand embroidery to kaftans, uniforms, caps and corporate wear.

Skills training

  • Hand embroidery stitches
  • Embroidery machine operation
  • Digitising designs
  • Thread and fabric choice
  • Bulk orders for schools and companies

Training path, step by step

  1. First stepPractise five hand embroidery stitches on scrap fabric to train your eye and hands.
  2. Learn stitches and threads
  3. Operate an embroidery machine
  4. Digitise a simple logo
  5. Complete orders with supervision
  6. Take school and corporate orders

Certification

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) for tailoring and related trades; portfolio and loyal customers.
