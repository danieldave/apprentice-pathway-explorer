Apprentice Tools

Quick answer: three free tools that protect apprentices and help artisans find customers. Everything is saved on your own phone.

A

Apprenticeship Agreement

Write a fair agreement with your master before you start: length, fee, hours and freedom terms. Our fairness check warns you about unfair terms.

Open B

Skill Passport

Tick off every skill as you learn it. Your master confirms with a PIN. Share a signed link as proof for customers, SUPA screening or a trade test.

Open C

Artisan Profile Card

Make a WhatsApp-ready business card and a mini web page with your skills, area and a QR code.
