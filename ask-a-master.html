Ask a Master: 10 questions before you join

Quick answer: before you agree to learn a trade, ask the master these 10 questions and write down the answers. A good master answers them gladly. Print the card or save it on your phone and take it with you.

📝 Write the agreement

ASK A MASTER10 questions before I join a workshop
  1. How long will my training last?Ask for the number of months or years. Compare it with the usual time on the trade page. If it is much longer, ask why.
  2. What will I pay, and when?Ask for the full fee, what it covers, and whether you can pay in parts. Ask what happens to the money if training stops early.
  3. What days and hours will I work?A fair workshop gives you rest days and time for church, mosque or school. Write the hours down.
  4. What exactly will I learn, and in what order?Ask the master to list the skills. Tick them off in your Skill Passport as you learn each one.
  5. Who will teach me: you or a senior apprentice?Both are normal. You should still get time with the master on the hard skills.
  6. Which safety gear will I use, and who provides it?Goggles, gloves, boots or a mask, depending on the trade. No gear means real danger. Do not start without it.
  7. Will you help me take a trade test or NSQ certificate?A certificate lets you prove your skill anywhere in Nigeria. Ask when you can sit for it.
  8. What does the freedom ceremony cost?Ask now, not at the end. Agree the amount and put it in the written agreement.
  9. Can I speak to someone you trained before?A good master is proud of past apprentices. Talk to one and ask how they were treated.
  10. Will we sign a written agreement with a witness?Use the free Apprenticeship Agreement tool. Your parent or guardian should sign too.

Tick each box when you have a clear answer. Fewer than 7 ticks? Visit another workshop before you decide. · Apprentice Pathways, Yewa College ByteBusters

Take a parent, guardian or older relative with you. This card is a guide, not legal advice.
