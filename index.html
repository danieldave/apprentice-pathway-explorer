    The journey

    How an apprenticeship works

    Every pathway on this site follows the same five stages, from your first choice to the day your master gives you your freedom.

    1. Choose

      Explore trades, careers and skills, and take the Pathway Finder to see what fits you.

    2. Find a master

      Visit workshops, ask questions and agree on terms in writing.

    3. Learn on the job

      Start with simple tasks and take on real work as your master trusts you more.

    4. Get certified

      Prove your skill with a trade test, NABTEB or NSQ certificate, or a strong portfolio.

    5. Freedom

      Graduate from your apprenticeship and work for yourself or an employer.

    Industrial Training Fund (ITF)

    Skill-Up Artisans (SUPA)

    What it is
    A national programme that screens, trains and certifies working artisans. The 2026 phase targets about 200,000 artisans.
    What you get
    Training, local and international certification, tools, business support, and exit routes into jobs, overseas work or business incubation.
    Who can apply
    Practising artisans. Applicants register online and attend screening in their senatorial district.
    Official website

    Federal Ministry of Education

    TVET Initiative

    What it is
    Free technical and vocational training in about 28 priority trades at approved centres across the country. Launched in 2025.
    What you get
    Practical training (about 90% hands-on) for 6 to 12 months, with a monthly stipend and post-training starter support.
    Who can apply
    Young people who apply through the national portal and are verified with their NIN.
    Official website

    Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation & Digital Economy with NITDA

    3 Million Technical Talent (3MTT)

    What it is
    Free training in tech skills such as software development, data analysis, cybersecurity, UI/UX and animation.
    What you get
    Mostly online training with in-person sessions, an average of three months per course, and placement support.
    Who can apply
    Nigerians who pass the entry assessment. There is no application fee.
    Official website
