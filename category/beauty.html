Questions people ask

Which beauty & personal care skill is quickest to learn?

Gele & Head-tie Styling: about 1–2 months.

Which beauty & personal care skill is cheapest to start?

Gele & Head-tie Styling (low start-up cost).

How long does beauty & personal care training take in Nigeria?

Between 1 month and 2–2 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for beauty & personal care?

Trade test for hairdressing where offered; portfolio, reviews and hygiene standards; NAFDAC registration for cosmetics you sell.
