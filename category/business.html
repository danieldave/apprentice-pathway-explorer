Business & Services

Quick answer: there are 4 business & services pathways here. The quickest is Laundry & Dry Cleaning (1–3 months); the cheapest to start is Bookkeeping & Small Business Accounts. Short courses, CAC business registration, and a record of reliable service.

Questions people ask

Which business & services skill is quickest to learn?

Laundry & Dry Cleaning: about 1–3 months.

Which business & services skill is cheapest to start?

Bookkeeping & Small Business Accounts (low start-up cost).

How long does business & services training take in Nigeria?

Between 1 month and 6 months, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for business & services?

Short courses, CAC business registration, and a record of reliable service.
