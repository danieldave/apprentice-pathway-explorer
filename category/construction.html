Questions people ask

Which building & construction skill is quickest to learn?

Interlocking Paving & Kerbs: about 3–6 months.

Which building & construction skill is cheapest to start?

Interlocking Paving & Kerbs (low start-up cost).

How long does building & construction training take in Nigeria?

Between 3 months and 3–3 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for building & construction?

Trade test (Grade III, II, I) through the Federal Ministry of Labour or your state examinations board; NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate.
