Questions people ask

Which automotive & transport skill is quickest to learn?

Professional Driving: about 1–3 months.

Which automotive & transport skill is cheapest to start?

Professional Driving (low start-up cost).

How long does automotive & transport training take in Nigeria?

Between 1 month and 4–4 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for automotive & transport?

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); dealer and brand workshops; FRSC licence for driving roles.
