Metalwork & Fabrication

Quick answer: there are 3 metalwork & fabrication pathways here. The quickest is Blacksmithing (1–2 years); the cheapest to start is Blacksmithing. Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; welding codes for industrial jobs.

Metalwork & Fabrication

Blacksmithing

Forge farm tools, knives, hoes, cutlasses and metal parts by heating and hammering steel.

Questions people ask

Which metalwork & fabrication skill is quickest to learn?

Blacksmithing: about 1–2 years.

Which metalwork & fabrication skill is cheapest to start?

Blacksmithing (low start-up cost).

How long does metalwork & fabrication training take in Nigeria?

Between 1–1 years and 3–3 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for metalwork & fabrication?

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; welding codes for industrial jobs.
