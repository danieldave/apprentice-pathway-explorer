Woodwork & Furniture

Quick answer: there are 2 woodwork & furniture pathways here. The quickest is Upholstery (1–2 years); the cheapest to start is Upholstery. Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; a portfolio of finished pieces.

Woodwork & Furniture

Questions people ask

Which woodwork & furniture skill is quickest to learn?

Upholstery: about 1–2 years.

Which woodwork & furniture skill is cheapest to start?

Upholstery (low start-up cost).

How long does woodwork & furniture training take in Nigeria?

Between 1–1 years and 3–3 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for woodwork & furniture?

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; a portfolio of finished pieces.
