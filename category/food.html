Questions people ask

Which food & hospitality skill is quickest to learn?

Small Chops & Pastry: about 1–2 months.

Which food & hospitality skill is cheapest to start?

Small Chops & Pastry (low start-up cost).

How long does food & hospitality training take in Nigeria?

Between 1 month and 1–1 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for food & hospitality?

Food handler training and NAFDAC registration for packaged products; trade test for catering where offered.
