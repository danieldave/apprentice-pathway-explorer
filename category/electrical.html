Questions people ask

Which electrical & energy skill is quickest to learn?

Satellite Dish & TV Installation: about 1–3 months.

Which electrical & energy skill is cheapest to start?

Satellite Dish & TV Installation (low start-up cost).

How long does electrical & energy training take in Nigeria?

Between 1 month and 3–3 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for electrical & energy?

Trade test (Grade III, II, I); NABTEB Modular Trade Certificate; manufacturer training for solar and inverter brands.
