Questions people ask

Which agriculture & agro-processing skill is quickest to learn?

Cassava & Grain Processing: about 1–3 months.

Which agriculture & agro-processing skill is cheapest to start?

Snail Farming (low start-up cost).

How long does agriculture & agro-processing training take in Nigeria?

Between 1 month and 1–1 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for agriculture & agro-processing?

Training from state agricultural development programmes (ADPs) and farm institutes; NAFDAC registration for packaged products.
