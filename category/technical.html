Questions people ask

Which technical & industrial skill is quickest to learn?

Laboratory & Food Quality Control: about 6–12 months.

Which technical & industrial skill is cheapest to start?

Laboratory & Food Quality Control (low start-up cost).

How long does technical & industrial training take in Nigeria?

Between 6 months and 3–3 years, depending on the trade.

What certificates are there for technical & industrial?

NABTEB or polytechnic certificates; company and safety certifications; professional registration where the law requires it.
