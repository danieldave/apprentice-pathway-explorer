Questions people ask

Do I need a written apprenticeship agreement in Nigeria?

Many apprenticeships are agreed by word of mouth, which causes problems about the length of training, fees and the freedom ceremony. A simple written agreement, signed by the master, the apprentice and a guardian or witness, protects everyone.

What should an apprenticeship agreement include?

Names of the master, apprentice and guardian; the trade; start date and length; days and hours; the fee and what happens if training ends early; feeding or transport support; the trade test plan; what the freedom ceremony costs and who pays; what the apprentice receives at freedom; and how disagreements will be settled.

How long should an apprenticeship last?

It depends on the trade. Each trade page shows the typical time, for example welding about 2 to 3 years. Our fairness check warns you if an agreement is much longer than usual.

Is this agreement a legal document?

It is a clear written record that people sign in front of witnesses. It is not legal advice. For large sums of money or disputes, talk to a lawyer, the trade association or a community leader.
