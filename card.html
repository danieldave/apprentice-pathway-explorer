Artisan Profile Card

Quick answer: make a picture card for your WhatsApp status and a mini web page with your skills, area and a QR code. Customers tap to chat with you.

Questions people ask

How do I advertise my skills as an artisan in Nigeria?

Make a clear card with your trade, skills, area and WhatsApp number; post it on your WhatsApp status and in community groups; ask happy customers to share it. A Skill Passport link builds extra trust.

Is my phone number safe?

Your card is made on your phone. Your number is only in the card image and link that you choose to share. Do not add a number you do not want customers to call.
