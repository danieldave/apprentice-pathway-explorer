Questions people ask

What is a Skill Passport?

A digital logbook of the skills you have learnt in your trade. You tick a skill when you can do it alone, and your master confirms it with a PIN. You can share a signed link or QR code.

Is a Skill Passport a certificate?

No. It is a record of practice confirmed by your master. Official certificates come from the trade test (Grade III, II, I), NABTEB or national programmes. A passport helps you prepare and shows customers and screeners what you can do.

How do people know my passport is real?

When you share it, our server signs the link. If anyone changes it, the page shows "changed after signing". Only skills your master confirmed with the PIN show as confirmed.
